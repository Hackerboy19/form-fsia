TL;DR

  • Missing

    59 live pages aren't in any sitemap. That includes 51 of the 64 state Miss/Mrs registration forms, the pages that should bring in this season's sign-ups.

  • Broken

    192 sitemap URLs don't return 200. 80 return 404, 76 return a 500 server error, and 32 redirect, 22 of them to the homepage.

  • Stale

    The sitemap is hand-written and frozen. No child sitemap has a lastmod, the index dates are from 2023–24, and only 3 of 5,519 URLs are 2026 content.

  • Canonical

    Bharat Couture Week's canonical points to a page that redirects to the homepage. Google will likely drop it from search.

  • On-page

    49 of 72 form titles are too long, 49 descriptions are too long, and 13 pages have two H1s. 71 pages also mark up a year-long "Event" at the Jaipur office.

  • Fix

    Replace the 25 static XML files with one sitemap generated from the database. That fixes missing pages, dead URLs, duplicates and dates together. The task list below starts with quick manual fixes for this week.

      Gap 5

      Structured data

      Every form page outputs valid JSON-LD. The problem is the Event block on 71 pages: it claims a 365-day event (2026-01-01 → 2026-12-31) at the Jaipur office, including on Miss Delhi and Miss Kerala. A registration window isn't an event under Google's guidelines, and misleading event markup can lead to a manual action.

      Option A (recommended): remove Event from form pages

      Keep Organization, WebPage, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage. Put Event only on pages for real dated events, such as auditions or the grand finale.

      Option B: describe each real audition

      One Event per audition with its real date, venue and city, plus offers.price, priceCurrency: "INR", availability and validFrom.
